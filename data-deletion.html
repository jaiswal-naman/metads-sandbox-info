Request data deletion

Ads Agent Sandbox is a personal development and testing project operated by Naman Jaiswal.

  1. Email namanjaiswalofficial@gmail.com with the subject “Ads Agent Sandbox data deletion”.
  2. Include the email associated with your use of the app and, if known, the relevant Meta account or Page ID. Explain whether you want to disconnect access, delete local app records, or both. Never send passwords or access tokens.
  3. The operator will review the request, verify that it concerns your data, and reply about its status and any information needed to complete it. This is a manual process; no automatic deletion callback is offered.

Relevant local records can include connection credentials, asset bindings, conversations, checkpoints, proposals, execution records and test files. Data held independently by Meta, OpenAI or Cloudflare is subject to those providers' own processes. Deleting local records does not delete Facebook Pages or Meta ad objects; requests affecting those assets need separate ownership and scope verification.

You may also remove this app's access through your Meta account's app or business-integration settings to stop future access.
